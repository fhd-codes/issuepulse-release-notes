# IssuePulse v1.3.1

**Released:** 23 November 2026
**Type:** Patch
**App:** [app.issuepulse.io](https://app.issuepulse.io)

## Summary

The app and the pricing page now only describe features that IssuePulse actually has or plans.

## Bug fixes

- Topic pages no longer show a "Reader map" box for a feature that isn't built.
- The pricing page now lists the right planned features for Pro and Business. Issue scores, sponsored vs. regular issues and CSV export are already free, so they no longer appear on the Pro card. The cards no longer list a weekly insights email or a custom domain for vote links.

## Upgrade notes

Nothing to do. Everything you have today stays free during early access.
