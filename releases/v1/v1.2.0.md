# IssuePulse v1.2.0

**Released:** 20 October 2026
**Type:** Minor
**App:** [app.issuepulse.io](https://app.issuepulse.io)

## TL;DR

IssuePulse now tells you which **issue** readers were reacting to, not just which topic. Log each send, paste that issue's links, and every vote is tied to it. Each issue gets a score, you can see how sponsored issues land against your regular ones, and the dashboard sums up your week. You can also download everything as a spreadsheet. All of it is free.

---

## Highlights

- **Issues.** Log each newsletter you send and copy its own set of links. Every vote and embed open is recorded against that issue.
- **Issue scores.** One number per issue, from -100 to +100, so a big send and a small one compare fairly.
- **Sponsored vs regular.** See whether sponsored issues score higher or lower than your normal ones.
- **This week.** A short summary on your dashboard: votes, top topic, what readers asked for more of, your best and weakest issue, and new comments.
- **Export.** Download your votes, comments and issues as CSV files for any spreadsheet.

---

## What's new

### Issues

- A new **Issues** page in the sidebar. Add an issue with its title, send date, a link to it, whether it was sponsored, and its main topic.
- Each issue's page has copy-ready links for **every** topic, in all four copy formats. Paste them instead of the topic's own links. They look and work the same for readers.
- Links copied from a topic page keep working exactly as before. They just aren't tied to an issue.
- Deleting an issue keeps its votes on their topics, and links already sent with it keep recording votes.

### Issue scores and sponsored issues

- An issue's score is positive votes minus negative votes, divided by all votes, from -100 to +100. It appears once 5 readers have voted on that issue.
- The issue page also shows the share of positive votes, embed opens, and votes for each link.
- **Sponsored vs regular** compares the average score of your sponsored issues with your regular ones, once you have at least 3 scored issues of each kind. Until then it tells you how many more it needs.

### Link sentiment

- Each link now says whether a vote on it counts for or against an issue: **Positive**, **Neutral** or **Negative**. Change it in **Edit topic**, next to each label.
- Existing links were set from their labels: "Loved this", "Useful" and "More on this" are positive; "Too long" and "Not relevant" are negative; any label you renamed is neutral until you choose.

### Dashboard

- A **This week** card sums up the last 7 days: total votes, your top topic, which topic readers asked for more of, your best and weakest issue, how sponsored issues compare, and up to 3 new comments. Below 20 votes it says it's too early to call a winner instead of naming one.

### Settings

- **Export your data**, under Settings → Workspace: download your votes, comments or issues as CSV. Reader comments that look like spreadsheet formulas are exported as plain text.

## Upgrade notes

Nothing to do. Links in issues you've already sent keep working. To start scoring issues, add your next issue on the **Issues** page and paste its links instead of the topic's.
