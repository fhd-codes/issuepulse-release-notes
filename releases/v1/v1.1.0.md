# IssuePulse v1.1.0

**Released:** 18 October 2026
**Type:** Minor
**App:** [app.issuepulse.io](https://app.issuepulse.io)

## TL;DR

IssuePulse now looks and talks like [issuepulse.io](https://www.issuepulse.io): same logo, colours and type, and help that lives in one place, the [help center](https://www.issuepulse.io/help), linked from every setting it explains. The trend chart's "All time" view really covers all time now, and embed opens are counted no matter which format a topic is set to.

---

## Highlights

- **All time means all time.** The trend chart shows every day since a topic was created. Long histories scroll sideways and open on today.
- **Embed opens always count.** Paste the Embed block from any topic and its opens are counted, even if the topic is set to Linked, Markdown or Plain.
- **Help where you need it.** Short "Learn more" links next to copy formats, link counts, labels, the URL lock, opens, the trend chart, comments, deleting a topic, your handle and your profile.
- **The IssuePulse brand, everywhere.** Logo in the sidebar, on sign-in and on the 404 page, a browser-tab icon, and the website's colours and headline type.

---

## What's new

### Topic detail page

- "Lifetime" is now **All time**. It covers every day since the topic was created, not just the last 90, and its totals match the stats at the top of the page.
- Past 90 days the chart scrolls sideways inside its card. It opens on today, fades the edge with more to see, and scrolls with touch, a trackpad or the arrow keys.
- The copy menu starts on the topic's own format and remembers your choice **per topic**.
- Topics set to Linked, Markdown or Plain show opens once their Embed block has been pasted and opened. Click-through stays an Embed-topic figure.

### Sidebar

- **Help center** and **What's new** links open the website.
- The release number now sits at the bottom of the sidebar and links here.

### Sign-up and settings

- Sign-up links to the Terms and the Privacy Policy.
- Profile settings explain how to delete your account.

### Reader thank-you page

- A small "Feedback by IssuePulse" line under the comment box. It opens the website in a new tab, so a half-written comment isn't lost.

---

## Improvements

- Small grey text is darker and easier to read, and orange links pass contrast checks.
- Copy-format hints use the same words as the help center.
- The embed block's "powered by IssuePulse" link now goes to issuepulse.io.

### Removed

- The in-app Guide page. Everything on it was already in the help center, which has more detail. Old Guide links now open the help center.

---

## Bug fixes

- The Comments total on a topic page no longer stops at 50.
- The chart legend says "1 vote" and "1 open" instead of "1 votes" and "1 opens".
- The trend chart can't repeat or skip a day when the clocks change.

---

## Upgrade notes

Nothing to do. Links in issues you've already sent keep working. Embed blocks you copy from now on link their "powered by" line to issuepulse.io.
