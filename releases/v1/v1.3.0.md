# IssuePulse v1.3.0

**Released:** 3 November 2026
**Type:** Minor
**App:** [app.issuepulse.io](https://app.issuepulse.io)

## TL;DR

The dashboard is faster, and it never leaves you staring at a frozen screen. Every page now shows a preview of itself the moment you click, while your numbers load in. Pages also load their data in fewer steps, so the numbers arrive sooner.

---

## Highlights

- **Instant feedback.** Click any page and its layout appears straight away, with grey placeholders where your numbers will go.
- **Faster pages.** The dashboard, topics, issues and settings pages fetch their data in fewer steps, so they finish loading sooner.
- **Search and buttons that show they're working.** Searching, filtering and sorting topics, and saving or deleting, now show that something is happening until the page has updated.

---

## Improvements

### Every page

- Each page in the dashboard shows a loading preview as soon as you open it, instead of waiting until everything is ready.
- The sidebar link you clicked shows a small marker while its page opens.
- Pages ask for their data in fewer steps, and the parts that don't depend on each other load together.

### Topics

- While search results, a filter, a new sort order or the next page load, the topic list dims and the search box shows a spinner.
- The format and sort menus keep the option you picked instead of briefly jumping back.

### Saving and deleting

- After you create, edit or delete a topic or an issue, or change your name or handle, the button stays busy until the page shows your change. Before, the dialog closed first and the page caught up a moment later.

### Signing in

- IssuePulse now remembers that you're signed in for a few minutes at a time, which saves a check on every page.
- So when you change your password, your other devices are signed out within 5 minutes instead of straight away. From the moment you change it they can't create, change, delete or export anything, and the device you changed it on stays signed in.
