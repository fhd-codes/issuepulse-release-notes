# IssuePulse v1.0.0

**Released:** 9 October 2026
**Type:** Major — first public release
**App:** [app.issuepulse.io](https://app.issuepulse.io)

## TL;DR

IssuePulse v1.0.0 is the first public release, live at [app.issuepulse.io](https://app.issuepulse.io). Create a topic, paste the generated feedback links into your newsletter footer, and every reader click becomes a vote you can read on your dashboard. Readers don't sign up and don't fill in a form.

This release adds a real dashboard home, a searchable topics table, a detail page for each topic with trend charts and reader comments, editable link labels, a choice of 3 or 5 links per topic, four copy formats, embed open counting, a setup guide, and a layout that works on phones.

---

## Highlights

- **One-click feedback links.** Each topic gets its own set of tracking links. A reader clicks one in your newsletter and the vote is recorded. That's it for them.
- **Your own link labels.** Rename every link to fit your newsletter, for example "Loved this", "Too long", or "More on this".
- **3 or 5 links per topic.** Use a shorter set when five options feel like too many. Switching back later keeps every vote.
- **Topic detail page.** A vote breakdown, a trend chart over 7, 14, 30 or 90 days or all time, and the comments readers left.
- **Embed open counting.** For embed-style topics, IssuePulse counts how often the block was opened, so you can compare opens with votes.
- **Works on your phone.** Every page works from small phone screens up to desktop.

## What's included

### For readers (the public vote page)

- Clicking a feedback link records the vote and shows a short thank-you page.
- Readers can add an optional comment after voting. Each vote can carry one comment.
- No account, no form and no JavaScript needed, so the links work inside email clients.
- Automated link scanners and email prefetchers are filtered out, so they don't count as votes.

### Dashboard home

- A welcome page with your totals and a quote of the day.
- If you have no topics yet, it points you to create your first one. Once you do, it takes you to your results.

### Topics

- **Create a topic** with a name, a URL slug (suggested automatically, editable), a copy format and a link count of 3 or 5.
- **Edit a topic**: name, slug, copy format, link count and every link label, all in one place.
- **Delete a topic**, with a confirmation step.
- **Topics table** with search, a copy-format filter, sorting and paging. Your filters stay in the URL, so you can bookmark or share a view. On small screens the table turns into cards.
- **Slug lock.** Once a topic has received votes, its slug can't be changed, so links you've already sent keep working.

### Topic detail page

- A breakdown of votes for each link.
- A trend chart for the last 7, 14, 30 or 90 days, or all time.
- Your tracking links, ready to copy, with a live preview of how they'll look.
- Reader comments left on the vote page.
- An embed click-through figure (votes compared with counted opens), capped at 100%.

### Copy formats

Pick the format that suits where you paste the links:

| Format | Best for |
|---|---|
| Embed | Newsletter tools that accept an HTML block. Also enables open counting. |
| Linked | Editors where you paste links as rich text. |
| Markdown | Markdown-based newsletter platforms. |
| Plain | Plain-text emails, or anywhere else. |

The format you last picked on a topic is remembered in your browser.

### Embed open counting

- Embed-format topics include a tiny invisible image that counts each time the block is opened.
- Scanners and prefetchers are filtered out.
- Repeated opens from the same reader within 5 minutes count once. This keeps email-client re-scans from inflating the number, while still counting separate reads.

### Guide

- A new in-app guide covering setup steps, the four copy formats, and answers to common questions.

### Settings

- **Workspace handle**: the first part of every tracking link you send.
- **Profile**: update your name and change your password. Changing your password signs you out on other devices.

### Accounts

- Sign up and sign in with email and password.
- You choose your workspace handle when you sign up.

## Improvements

- **Mobile-ready layout.** The sidebar becomes a slide-out menu on smaller screens, tables become cards, tap targets are larger, and form fields no longer make iOS Safari zoom in when you tap them.
- **Consistent look across pages.** Buttons, cards, badges, stats, empty states and dialogs now share one design.
- **Accessible dialogs.** Keyboard focus stays inside an open dialog, Escape closes it, and focus returns to where you were when it closes.
- **Colour-coded vote badges** on the vote page and the dashboard match each other.
- **Older links keep working.** Links created before link labels became editable (for example ending in `/loved` or `/useful`) still record votes against the right option.

### Removed

These were removed because they weren't connected to any working feature:

- The global search box and notification bell in the top bar. Search now lives on the Topics page.
- The Google and Apple sign-in buttons. Sign-in is by email and password.
- The "Billing & Payments" menu item.
- Two broken links on the "page not found" screen.

---

## Bug fixes

- Typing in the create or edit topic form no longer moves the cursor out of the field after every character.
- The topics search box no longer loses focus while you type.
- Deleting a topic from its own page now takes you back to your topics list.
- Trend chart labels are now readable on phones.
- Trend totals now match the stats shown above the chart.
- The click-through figure no longer shows values above 100%.
- The password mismatch message now shows a proper apostrophe instead of a raw code.
- Choosing a copy format other than Embed and reloading the page no longer causes an error.
- The mobile menu no longer tells screen readers it's open while it's closed.
- Dashboard pages no longer error when your session has expired. You are sent to the sign-in page instead.
- Menu items, cards and navigation no longer show in the link colour by mistake.

## Security

- Topic names and other text on the public vote page are now escaped, so they can't run code in a reader's browser.
- Read-only workspaces are now enforced on every change to topics and on handle changes.
- Opening a topic that belongs to another workspace returns "not found".

## Upgrade notes

Nothing to do. Sign in at [app.issuepulse.io](https://app.issuepulse.io) as usual. All existing topics and votes were moved over automatically, and links you've already sent keep working.
