# IssuePulse v1.1.1

**Released:** 19 October 2026
**Type:** Patch
**App:** [app.issuepulse.io](https://app.issuepulse.io)

## Summary

Your workspace settings are now protected, and your handle locks once readers start using your links, so links in sent issues can't break by accident.

## Bug fixes

- **Your handle locks once your links are in use.** As soon as a reader votes on one of your links, or opens one of your Embed blocks, your workspace handle can no longer be changed. Every link you've sent starts with your handle, so changing it would have stopped all of them recording votes. Topic URLs already worked this way. Settings shows the handle with a lock and a link to the [help article](https://www.issuepulse.io/help/account/change-your-handle).

## Security

- Your workspace's handle, members and the workspace itself can now only be changed through the IssuePulse app's own settings, which apply every rule (such as reserved handles and the lock above).

## Upgrade notes

Nothing to do. If you still want a different handle and haven't sent any links yet, change it in **Settings** before your first issue goes out.
